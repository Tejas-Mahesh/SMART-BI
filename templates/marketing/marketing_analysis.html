ANALYTICAL METHODOLOGY

How Marketing Intelligence Works

Smart BI evaluates marketing investment using spend, revenue, conversions, clicks, impressions and customer acquisition data. ROI measures the return relative to marketing investment, while ROAS measures revenue generated for every unit of advertising spend. Campaigns and channels are classified using relative performance benchmarks.
